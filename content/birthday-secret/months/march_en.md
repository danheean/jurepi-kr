## March Birthstone

The birthstone for March is the **aquamarine**, a stone that captures the very essence of the sea. Its name means "water of the sea" in Latin, and throughout history, seafarers have treasured aquamarine for its protective power on long voyages. This pale, crystalline blue-green gemstone has been worn by mariners, explorers, and travelers seeking safe passage and clear judgment.

With a hardness of 7.5 to 8, aquamarine is quite durable and takes a beautiful polish. Fine specimens come from Brazil and Nigeria, though smaller high-quality stones are found in many regions. The gemstone is believed to calm turbulent emotions and sharpen mental clarity—qualities especially fitting for a month of transition and renewal. People born in March are said to possess aquamarine's transparency and courage, the ability to see situations clearly and act with conviction even when the path ahead is uncertain.

In medieval times, aquamarine was inscribed with symbols of hope and given as a wedding gift to ensure marital happiness and safe journeys together.

## March Birth Flowers

March brings the **forsythia** and the **cherry blossom**.

The **forsythia** announces spring's true arrival with masses of bright yellow flowers that seem to glow along bare branches. It symbolizes anticipation, joy, and the triumph of hope after a long winter.

The **cherry blossom** is fleeting and ethereal, appearing for just a brief window before falling like snow. Across cultures, it represents the beauty of impermanence, deep love, and spiritual perfection—the reminder that beauty need not last forever to be profound.

## March Birth Color

March's birth color is **pale aquamarine blue-green**, a shade that merges sky and sea. This color speaks of clarity, renewal, and the courage to move forward into new beginnings. It captures March's essence: the moment spring truly arrives and life awakens again.
