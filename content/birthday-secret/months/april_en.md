## April Birthstone

The birthstone for April is the **diamond**, the most coveted gemstone of all. A diamond is the hardest natural substance on Earth, with a hardness of 10 on the Mohs scale. It symbolizes eternal love, unbreakable bonds, and the permanence of true commitment. Formed deep within the Earth over billions of years under extreme temperature and pressure, each diamond tells a story of extraordinary resilience.

Diamonds are mined primarily from South Africa, Russia, and Botswana, though they are found in select locations worldwide. The most prized are those with exceptional clarity and brilliance—stones that catch and refract light in spectacular fashion. Beyond their beauty, diamonds represent the triumph of extreme conditions creating something perfect and timeless. People born in April are said to possess diamond-like qualities: strength, clarity of purpose, and an inner light that shines through adversity.

In modern times, the diamond has become inseparable from proposals and marriage—a symbol of love intended to last forever, just as a diamond itself will endure.

## April Birth Flowers

April's flowers are the **cherry blossom** and the **tulip**.

The **cherry blossom** reaches its glorious peak in spring, carpeting entire landscapes with pink and white blossoms. It represents the beauty of life's fleeting moments and the perfection found in transience.

The **tulip** comes in an extraordinary palette of colors—crimson, gold, pink, purple, and bicolors. Each color carries its own meaning, though all tulips represent elegance, grace, and perfect love. These structured, graceful flowers embody spring's renewal and hope.

## April Birth Color

April's birth color is **clear crystal white**, the transparency of diamond itself. This color represents purity, strength, and absolute clarity. It speaks of truth, new beginnings, and the crystalline perfection of genuine connection.
