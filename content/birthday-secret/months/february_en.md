## February Birthstone

The birthstone for February is the **amethyst**, a violet gemstone that has fascinated cultures worldwide. Its name carries an ancient story: the Greeks believed it prevented drunkenness, and indeed the word itself means "not intoxicated." Kings and clergy wore amethyst rings, convinced the stone kept the mind sharp and the spirit serene.

With a hardness of 7, amethyst is among the most durable quartz varieties. The finest specimens come from Brazil and Uruguay, each region producing shades of purple ranging from pale lavender to deep violet. Amethyst has long been considered a stone for meditation and clarity, calming the mind and soothing turbulent emotions. Many who seek spiritual growth keep an amethyst cluster on their bedside table or meditation altar.

People born in February are said to possess the amethyst's qualities: thoughtful, composed, and grounded even in chaos. They are the ones who pause before speaking, who listen deeply, and who find strength in stillness rather than noise.

## February Birth Flowers

February's flowers are the **freesia** and the **plum blossom**.

The **freesia** brings cheerful yellow blooms and a delicate, sweet fragrance to late winter. Its flowers symbolize kindness, innocence, and trustworthiness—gentle qualities that warm the coldest season.

The **plum blossom**, steeped in East Asian tradition, represents perseverance and noble character. It blooms in severe cold, teaching us that beauty can flourish even in hardship. Ancient poets and scholars immortalized the plum blossom as a symbol of integrity and grace under pressure.

## February Birth Color

February's birth color is **violet**—a shade of contemplation and spiritual depth. This color balances passion with introspection, wisdom with humility. Like the amethyst itself, violet speaks of transformation and the clarity that comes from looking inward.
