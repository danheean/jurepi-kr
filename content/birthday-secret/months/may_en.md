## May Birthstone

The birthstone for May is the **emerald**, a stone of rich, vibrant green that embodies hope, renewal, and love. Emeralds have been treasured for millennia; Cleopatra herself was famously devoted to emeralds and even had her portrait carved into the stone. In ancient Rome, the emerald was believed to sharpen the mind and improve memory.

With a hardness of 7.5 to 8, emerald is relatively durable, though more fragile than diamond or sapphire. The finest emeralds come from Colombia and Zambia, each region producing its own characteristic shade of green. The color comes from chromium and vanadium within the beryl crystal structure, creating that distinctive life-filled green. Emerald is believed to calm the heart, deepen friendships, and strengthen romantic bonds. Those born in May are said to possess the emerald's qualities: deep feeling, genuine compassion, and a connection to growth and renewal.

In medieval times, emerald was prescribed for eye strain—people believed gazing upon an emerald would soothe tired eyes and restore clear vision.

## May Birth Flowers

May brings the **rose**, the **carnation**, and the **lily of the valley**.

The **rose** is the undisputed symbol of love, beauty, and passion—no flower speaks more eloquently to the human heart.

The **carnation** represents maternal affection and gratitude, carrying the warmth of a mother's endless devotion.

The **lily of the valley** is delicate and sweet-scented, bearing small white bell-shaped flowers. It symbolizes purity, happiness, and the return of contentment.

## May Birth Color

May's birth color is **vibrant fresh green**, the color of spring growth and new life. This shade represents renewal, vitality, and the deep, nurturing love that sustains all living things. It captures May's essence of flourishing potential and genuine human connection.
