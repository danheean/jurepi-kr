## June Birthstone

The birthstone for June is the **pearl**, a gemstone born not from the depths of the earth, but from the sea. A pearl forms when an irritant—often a grain of sand—finds its way into an oyster or mussel. Rather than being damaged by this intrusion, the mollusk secretes layers of nacre (mother-of-pearl) around the irritant, transforming pain into beauty. This makes the pearl a powerful symbol of resilience and the capacity to find grace in adversity.

Pearls have long represented health, longevity, wealth, and purity across many cultures, particularly in Asia where they have been treasured for thousands of years. With a relative softness of 2.5 to 4.5 on the Mohs scale, pearls are typically cultivated rather than mined. The finest cultured pearls come from Japan, China, and the South Pacific, each region producing pearls with distinct colors and luster—from classic white to lustrous black, warm gold, and delicate rose.

Those born in June are said to possess pearl-like qualities: grace under pressure, quiet elegance, and an inner radiance that glows softly but distinctly.

## June Birth Flowers

June brings the **rose** and the **lily**.

The **rose** in full summer bloom represents deep love and passionate beauty—no flower rivals its symbolic resonance with the human heart.

The **lily** stands for purity, majesty, and refined grace. Its large, elegant blooms and rich fragrance make it one of the most prized cut flowers, capable of filling an entire room with its sweet, complex scent.

## June Birth Color

June's birth color is **lustrous pearl white**—a soft, iridescent shade that suggests both purity and subtle depth. This color represents grace, elegance, and the quiet strength found in vulnerability. It speaks of transformation and the beauty that emerges when we face our challenges with composure and grace.
