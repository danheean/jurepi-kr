## July Birthstone

The birthstone for July is the **ruby**, the king of gemstones. A ruby is actually a red variety of corundum—the same mineral that produces sapphires in other colors—but red corundum is far rarer and more precious. Rubies symbolize passion, love, courage, and regal dignity. Throughout history, rubies have adorned the crowns of emperors and the fingers of warriors.

With a hardness of 9 on the Mohs scale, rubies are second only to diamonds in durability. The finest rubies come from Myanmar, Thailand, and Sri Lanka, each region producing gems with slightly different characteristics. The red color comes from chromium within the crystal structure, creating that distinctive deep, glowing crimson. Ancient warriors wore rubies believing they would grant courage in battle and protect against wounds.

People born in July are said to possess the ruby's qualities: passionate conviction, noble strength, and an intense inner fire. Rubies have also been chosen for engagement rings by those who equate love with passion and unbending devotion.

## July Birth Flowers

July brings the **lotus** and the **lily**.

The **lotus** is sacred across many cultures, blooming pristinely from muddy waters. It symbolizes purity, enlightenment, and the triumph of the spirit over material challenges.

The **lily** in July's warm sunlight reaches peak elegance, representing majesty, purity, and timeless grace. Its fragrance carries the essence of summer abundance.

## July Birth Color

July's birth color is **deep ruby red**, a shade of intense passion and warmth. This color represents courage, love, and the vital energy that propels life forward. It captures July's essence: the height of summer, burning bright and full of life.
