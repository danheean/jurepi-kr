## August Birthstone

The birthstone for August is the **peridot**, a stone of pale yellow-green that glows as if lit from within. It symbolizes marital happiness, harmony, and prosperity. The name "peridot" comes from the Arabic word "faridat," meaning "gem," and the stone itself is a variety of olivine—the same mineral found in volcanic rocks on the moon.

With a hardness of 6.5 to 7, peridot is moderately durable and found primarily in the United States and Myanmar. Interestingly, historians believe that some of the emeralds Cleopatra prized so highly may actually have been peridots. The stone was particularly revered during the Middle Ages, when clergy wore peridot rings as symbols of their devotion and priestly duty.

Peridot is believed to bring peace, restful sleep, and renewed commitment between partners. Those born in August are said to inherit peridot's qualities: brightness, optimism, and the ability to strengthen the bonds of love and friendship through genuine warmth and kindness.

## August Birth Flowers

August brings the **sunflower** and the **morning glory**.

The **sunflower** tracks the sun's journey across the sky, symbolizing loyalty, longevity, and joy. Its large, golden face is summer itself captured in a single bloom.

The **morning glory** blooms fresh each dawn, representing bonds of love and new beginnings. A different flower graces each morning, reminding us that beauty is renewed daily.

## August Birth Color

August's birth color is **bright chartreuse green**—the color of new growth and optimism. This shade represents renewal, happiness, and the peaceful flourishing of genuine connection. It captures August's warmth and the contentment found in life's simple blessings.
