## About These Songs

**Lee Moon-sae – Living in This World (이 세상 살아가다 보면)** is a signature ballad by Lee Moon-sae, one of the defining voices of Korean pop in the 1980s–90s. Its gentle melody and comforting lyrics make it a favorite for quiet, tiring nights.

**Kim Min-jong – A Start at the End of the World (세상 끝에서의 시작)** is the OST of the 1998 SBS drama "Mister Q" and a signature song by actor-singer Kim Min-jong — a tender, emotional example of the classic 1990s Korean ballad.

**Gaho – Start (시작)** is OST Part.2 of the 2020 Korean drama "Itaewon Class." With lyrics about the courage to begin again and a powerful vocal, it became an anthem far beyond the show.

**Richard Marx & Donna Lewis – At the Beginning** is the theme song from the 1997 animated film "Anastasia." A soaring duet about new beginnings and love, it is a classic of movie-soundtrack pop.

**Hugh Grant & Drew Barrymore – Way Back into Love** is from the 2007 romantic comedy "Music and Lyrics." This duet about finding the way back to love captures the film's warm, playful mood.

**Christopher & CHUNG HA – Bad Boy** is a 2020 duet by Korean singer CHUNG HA and Danish pop star Christopher — an addictive, moody pop track that found fans both in Korea and abroad.

**Anne-Marie – 2002** is Anne-Marie's 2018 pop hit. Interpolating early-2000s pop, it is a breezy, nostalgic throwback to the year 2002.

**Poco – Sea of Heartbreak** is a country-rock classic by the American band Poco; the original is Don Gibson's 1961 song. An oldies favorite about a "sea of heartbreak," it has been loved for decades.
