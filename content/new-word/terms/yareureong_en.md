## Description

**Yareureong** doesn't really mean anything on its own — it's a meme built
entirely around how funny the mashed-up **sound** is. Take the already
trendy interjection "yareu" (roughly "yay!" or "woo!"), glue it to EXO's
hit song title "Growl" (으르렁), and you get a word people share on X
(Twitter) and online communities with reactions like "this is genuinely
low-brow but I can't stop laughing."

A similarly constructed pun, **halleluyareu** (blending "hallelujah" with
"yareu"), is often mentioned alongside it. Both are less about meaning
and more about **enjoying how awkward and silly they sound out loud**.
