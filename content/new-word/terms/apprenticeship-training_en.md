## Description

**Apprenticeship training** is learning by working next to a skilled
practitioner and picking up the craft through **observation, imitation,
and repetition**. Because you learn **in the real context of the work**
rather than from lectures or documents, it is especially good at
transferring the **tacit knowledge** that is hard to fully articulate.

- **Medieval guilds**: skills passed down through master–journeyman–apprentice ranks
- **Modern organizations**: OJT (on-the-job training) and mentor–mentee pairing
- **Software**: Software Craftsmanship and the book *Apprenticeship Patterns*

The key idea is **learning by working side by side**: expert and learner
solve problems together, building intuition through feedback and trial
and error.
