## Description

**Tacit knowledge** is what you know through body and experience but
cannot fully explain — like riding a bike, recognizing a face, or making a
skilled, intuitive judgment. Its opposite, **explicit knowledge**, can be
stated clearly in language, formulas, and documents.

Management scholar **Ikujiro Nonaka** described how the two continually
convert into each other in his **SECI model** of organizational knowledge
creation:

- **Socialization**: tacit → tacit (shared through joint experience)
- **Externalization**: tacit → explicit (captured as concepts and docs)
- **Combination**: explicit → explicit (combining existing knowledge)
- **Internalization**: explicit → tacit (absorbed through learning)

Turning individual and organizational know-how into documents, rules, and
automation — that is, making tacit knowledge explicit — is a central
challenge in both knowledge management and engineering.
