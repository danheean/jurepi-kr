---
term: Goneung (High-Ability)
tags: [buzzword, internet, fandom]
definition: |
  From "고지능" (high intelligence): being smart and impressively skilled at getting things done.
  "고능하다" (adjective) praises someone's sharp judgment and competence; "고능아" is the noun for such a person.
examples:
  - "The concept for this plan is so goneung—how did they even come up with it?"
  - "Sticking to a study schedule every single day? Total goneung-a."
reading: /ko.nɯŋ/
aliases: [goneung-hada, goneung-a, high-intelligence]
origin: Derived from "고지능" (high intelligence); spread through game streams and online communities. Its antonym is "저능하다" (low ability).
---

"고능하다" (adjective) and "고능아" (noun) are used together.

Because "고능아" plays on the derogatory "저능아" (low-ability), some dislike the echo—but in fan and
community usage it is usually **sincere, playful praise** for someone's cleverness or efficiency.
