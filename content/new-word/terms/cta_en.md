## Description

A **CTA** is a phrase or button that spells out, in a concrete verb,
exactly what a user should do next — so they never have to guess. "Start
free for 30 days" tends to outperform generic wording like "Submit"
because it lets the user **picture the outcome**.

### How to use it

- **Highlight only one CTA per screen.** Multiple competing CTAs make
  users hesitate and often leave without clicking either.
- **Start with a verb** and pair it with the payoff — "Start your free
  30-day trial" beats plain "Subscribe."
- Place at least one CTA **above the fold**, so it's visible without
  scrolling.
- **A/B test** the wording, color, and placement — it's the most common
  way teams improve click-through rate in practice.
