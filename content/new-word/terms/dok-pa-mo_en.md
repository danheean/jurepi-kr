term: Dokpamo (Sovereign AI Foundation Model)
tags: [AI, sovereign AI, foundation model, policy]
definition: |
  Korean abbreviation of "독자 AI 파운데이션 모델" (independent AI foundation model):
  a large-scale AI base model that a country builds and controls on its own, free of
  dependence on foreign vendors or licenses. It is the centerpiece of Korea's sovereign-AI strategy.
examples:
  - "The government spent ₩530 billion to select 'national champion' teams to build a dokpamo."
  - "To cut reliance on foreign models, calls grew for a sovereign AI like the dokpamo."
reading: Dokpamo
aliases: [independent AI foundation model, sovereign AI foundation model, K-foundation model, national champion AI]
origin: Spread in 2025 as the short form of Korea's government-funded "independent AI foundation model" program (Ministry of Science and ICT / NIPA).
---

A "foundation model" is a large AI trained on vast data that serves as the base for many
tasks. **Dokpamo** prepends "독자적" ("one's own / independent"), stressing that the model is
**built, owned, and controlled by the nation itself** rather than by a foreign company.

The underlying idea is **sovereign AI** — securing AI capability across data, infrastructure,
models, and applications at the national level, without depending on a specific overseas
vendor or license structure.

In Korea, the Ministry of Science and ICT and NIPA back competing "national champion AI"
teams with a ₩530 billion program (selecting five teams in August 2025 and narrowing them
through staged evaluations), releasing results as **open weights**. The goal is a Korean AI
model that ranks among the world's best.
